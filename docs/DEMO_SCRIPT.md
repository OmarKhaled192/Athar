# ATHAR — 75-second demo script

**0:00 — Home (10 s).** "ATHAR is a memorial garden for NASA robots that went quiet but kept teaching. Four real explorers, two ways in." Point at the four glowing stones: Eagle, Pioneer 10, Opportunity, Ingenuity. Use the language switch to introduce Arabic narration and right-to-left direction.

**0:10 — Start the Story (30 s).** Click **Start the Story**. Step to **Eagle**: *"Notice the badges. 'Creative narration' is the robot's voice; 'NASA-sourced' is the verified fact."* Drag the 3D model. Press **Listen**. Answer both quiz questions, then continue to **Opportunity**. *"Short chunks: one fact, one science takeaway."* At the finale, show the Garden Keeper score and printable teacher summary.

**0:40 — Explore the Garden (30 s).** Click **Explore the Garden**. *"Moon on the left, Earth far away, Mars on the right."* Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Click **Mars** filter, then tap **Ingenuity**. In the panel: timeline, story, science cards, **Ask the notes → "How many times did you fly?"**, NASA source link. Close, press **Deep space**, tap **Pioneer 10**.

**1:10 — Close (5 s).** *"Note the honesty: Eagle's ascent stage is in lunar orbit with 'fate unknown', not on the surface; Pioneer 10 is nowhere near Mars or the Moon; the scene says 'not to scale'. ATHAR is independent and not endorsed by NASA."*

## What judges should notice
1. Two **distinct** modes (guided vs free).
2. **Facts vs. narration** labelled everywhere; every record links to NASA.
3. **Scientific guardrails** (Ingenuity ≠ rover, Eagle = ascent stage only, Pioneer 10 = deep space).
4. Works for kids on phones: short text, big targets, keyboard + screen-reader friendly, reduced-motion, no-WebGL fallback.
5. Everything rendered from `data/characters.json`; add an explorer without touching the UI.
